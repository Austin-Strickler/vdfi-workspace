## Literature scan

Checked against the 78 filenames in `docs/literature/` and the papers carried in earlier briefings. Library note: neither Weiss 2021 nor any Weiss 2025 paper is in `docs/literature/` — Weiss 2021 is the anchor for Paper III and has been cited throughout `GOALS.md`, so it's worth adding. **Only one paper this week is 2026-dated** (the one you already downloaded); the rest are 2023–2025.

**Recent:**

1. **Zhang 2026b** — Constraining the nature of active galactic nuclei through circumgalactic Lyα emission at z = 2–3 (arXiv:2604.25127, April 2026). Read from the PDF you saved on 10-04 (abstract only). KCWI Lyα nebulae around 59 unobscured and 26 obscured quasars: unobscured systems are more asymmetric and larger (scale length r_h = 10.7 ± 0.5 kpc vs ≈6.6–7.7 kpc) and show steeply declining velocity-dispersion profiles (−4.3 ± 0.4 km s⁻¹ kpc⁻¹), read as feedback-driven outflows; this sits in tension with the orientation-only unified model. Two uses: it is a concrete template for what an undetected AGN residual would do to an inner bin (larger, asymmetric, dispersion falling outward), which is exactly the completeness statement `GOALS.md` §8 item 6 says doesn't yet exist; and its σ(r) slope is a comparison for the Hubble-flow line-broadening prediction in `TODO.md`. (Filename suggestion: `Zhang 2026b - ...` to avoid colliding with the CLAMATO paper already filed as `Zhang 2026`.)

**Older, likely not yet reviewed:**

2. **Weiss 2025** — Using Lyman Alpha Absorption to Measure the Intensity and Variability of z ~ 2.4 Ultraviolet Background Light (arXiv:2504.13253, April 2025). Stacks over 400,000 HETDEX LAE spectra and argues that HETDEX's sky subtraction over-subtracts UV light absorbed by neutral hydrogen in the LAE halos, leaving a negative signal that tracks the z≈2.4 UV background (12.9 ± 3.7 nW m⁻² sr⁻¹ at 4134 Å), with a dependence on local galaxy density. This is the main find of the week: the only published treatment I found of how sky subtraction reshapes a smooth component around a VIRUS stack. Directly relevant to What's next item 2.

3. **Khanlari 2025** — The HETDEX Survey: Probing neutral hydrogen in the circumgalactic medium of ~88,000 Lyman Alpha Emitters (arXiv:2507.15942, July 2025). Stacks HETDEX spectra around ~88,000 LAEs at 1.9<z<3.5 and detects neutral-hydrogen absorption out to ~350 kpc, building an empirical radial equivalent-width profile that is modestly higher than earlier work and resembles simulations. The absorption-side partner to the emission profile — a second, independent radial constraint from the same instrument on where gas sits relative to R_vir, and a possible cross-check on the one-halo/two-halo transition (Rakic 2012 is the only absorption comparison currently in the library). Plans a split by density, which is the environment axis of Paper II.

4. **Davis 2023** — HETDEX Public Source Catalog 1 — Stacking 50K Lyman Alpha Emitters (arXiv:2307.03096, July 2023). Stacks ~50,000 HETDEX LAE spectra (1.9<z<3.5) to describe the average emitter: blue, low-dust, young (≈5–15 Myr), ≈0.2–0.3 Z☉, with possible ionizing-photon leakage. Cited in `GOALS.md` §2 as a competitor but not in the library; useful context for the Paper III valve/engine discussion and for how a HETDEX-only stack handles the same continuum-subtraction problems you do.

5. **Kikuta 2023b** — UV & Lyα halos of Lyα emitters across environments at z=2.84 (arXiv:2302.12848, February 2023). Subaru HSC stack of 3,490 LAEs: two-component exponential Lyα fits (scale lengths ≈1.6 and 10.4 kpc), very large halos in protocluster cores (attributed to source overlap and diffuse emission), unclear environment trends elsewhere, and the first "UV halos" around bright emitters. Relevant to Paper II's environment prediction and as a caution on the source-overlap confound in overdense regions — which also affects your two-halo term. (Library already has `Kikuta 2023` on QSO stacks; use `Kikuta 2023b`.)
