## Literature scan

Checked against all 55 filenames in `docs/literature/` and against the standing suggestions carried in the 07-10 / 07-13 / 07-17 / 07-21 / 07-22 briefings (Carr 2025, Cerdosino 2025, Claeyssens 2022, Davis 2023, Gagnon-Hartman 2026, Li 2025, Runnholm 2023, Sebastian 2026, Smirnov 2026, Verhamme 2018, Wu 2020, Xue 2017, Yu 2025 — none re-listed). Weighted deliberately toward this cycle's actual theme: **faint-CGM-emission stacking where the background estimate is the measurement**, and modelling the intrinsic vs. observed halo profile.

**Recent:**

1. **Roy 2026** — Lighting Up the CGM: Strong, Jet-Aligned Hα Emission around Radio Galaxies (arXiv:2606.30088, June 2026). Stacks DESI background-quasar spectra along sightlines through the CGM of radio galaxies and detects a >5σ Hα excess confined to the collimated jet axis (θ < 20°), with intermediate- and large-angle sightlines showing nothing comparable. Methodologically it is the same problem this project is in the middle of — a faint CGM line recovered only in the stack, where the answer depends entirely on how the comparison/background sightlines are defined — and the angular control (on-axis vs. off-axis) is a clean template for the kind of null the `BG_HALVES` azimuthal split is built to provide. Also of direct interest to Paper III, which needs Balmer-line photon accounting.

2. **Pharo 2024** — The Intrinsic Distribution of Lyman-α Halos (arXiv:2409.04537, September 2024). Builds a 3D spatial–spectral forward model of Lyα halos parameterized by six properties (halo and compact-source scale lengths, halo flux fraction, compact ellipticity, spectral width, spectral skewness) and fits it to MUSE IFU observations to recover the *intrinsic* rather than observed halo distribution. Relevant on two axes: it is a same-instrument-class (IFU) forward-modelling analog to the PSF-aware profile fit, and its explicit separation of intrinsic from observed distributions is the argument this project will need when quoting `h1` with a PSF-width systematic band rather than a point value.

**Older, likely not yet reviewed:**

3. **Weiss 2024** — Absorption Troughs of Lyα Emitters in HETDEX (arXiv:2401.02490, ApJ 962, 102, January 2024). Stacks roughly half a million LAEs from HETDEX DR3 and finds clear absorption wings extending ~2000 km/s both redward and blueward of the Lyα line, attributed to large-scale neutral hydrogen in LAE environments. Same instrument, same survey, same stacking regime — and directly load-bearing for this project's background question: a broad absorption feature around Lyα in the *stack* is exactly the kind of structure that a background annulus sitting at 660–743 kpc could be imprinting or removing. Worth reading before interpreting the §1.1 rest-frame background stack.

4. **Gallego 2021** — Constraining the cosmic UV background at z > 3 with MUSE Lyman-α emission observations (arXiv:2103.09250, MNRAS 504, 16, March 2021). Searches for fluorescent Lyα from self-shielded clouds in deep MUSE data, and in the process states the failure mode this cycle's entire §2.5 effort is designed around: extended faint halos that fall below the noise are partially subtracted *as sky*, and the effect is **enhanced by stacking**, to a magnitude comparable to the expected signal. That is the most direct published statement of the "the annulus contains the signal" problem in `background-diagnostics.md` §0, and it belongs in the Methods citation list whichever way the variant test comes out.
