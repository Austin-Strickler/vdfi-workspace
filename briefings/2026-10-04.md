**Older, likely not yet reviewed:**

1. **Goovaerts 2024** — Charting the Lyman-α escape fraction in the range 2.9 < z < 6.7 and consequences for the LAE reionisation contribution (arXiv:2408.00517, August 2024). Uses gravitationally lensed galaxies with MUSE and JWST to measure Lyα escape fractions, finding negligible redshift evolution for individual galaxies but a strong rise toward fainter UV magnitude, with faint z~6 galaxies plausibly letting nearly all Lyα photons escape. VDFI's MOSDEF sample sits at the massive, low-escape end (Weiss 2021's ≲1%), so this is the opposite-end anchor for `GOALS.md` §6's f_esc dynamic-range check.

2. **Smith 2022** — The physics of Lyman-α escape from disc-like galaxies (arXiv:2111.13721, November 2021; accepted September 2022). Radiation-hydrodynamics plus radiative transfer on a Milky Way simulation; reprocessing LyC into recombination lines needs ~27.5% pre-absorption by dust, ~8.7% helium ionization and ~7.9% anisotropic escape, leaving only ~56% available for hydrogen lines. Directly relevant to how the Balmer-line "intrinsic Lyα" in Paper III's photon budget is derived from Hα: a case-B conversion without these terms would overstate the budget and so overstate the "missing" fraction.
