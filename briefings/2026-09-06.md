## Literature scan

Checked against all 72 filenames in `docs/literature/` and against the standing suggestions carried in the 07-10 through 08-30 briefings (Arrigoni Battaia 2016, Bennett 2026, Carr 2025, Cerdosino 2025, Claeyssens 2022, Davis 2023, Feldmeier 2013, Firestone 2026, Gagnon-Hartman 2026, Gallego 2021, Gronke 2015, Hall 2026, Kikuchihara 2022, Kikuta 2023 (HSC), Laferte-Urrutia 2026, Li 2025, Li & Zheng 2025, Liu 2024, Lujan Niemeyer 2026, Matsuda 2012, Roy 2026, Runnholm 2023, Sebastian 2026, Smirnov 2026, Verhamme 2018, Weiss 2024, Wu 2020, Xue 2017, Yu 2025, Zhang 2024 (MAMMOTH), Zhang 2026 (LAMOST), Zhang & Zaritsky 2024 — none re-listed). Weighted toward recommendation 1: this week's work is instrumental, so two of the four are about measuring an extended PSF and about the same instrument's outer-radius systematics.

**Recent:**

1. **Mirza Khanlari 2025** — The HETDEX Survey: Probing neutral hydrogen in the circumgalactic medium of ~88,000 Lyman Alpha Emitters (arXiv:2507.15942, July 2025; ApJ 989, 169, August 2025). Stacks millions of VIRUS fiber spectra around ~88,000 HETDEX LAEs at 1.9 < z < 3.5 to detect *absorption* — HI in the LAE halo scattering Lyα from the integrated background-galaxy light along the line of sight — out to a projected **~350 kpc** at z~2.5, and builds an empirical radial W_λ(Lyα) profile compared against simulations. This is the same instrument, the same redshift range, and the same outer-radius regime as VDFI's two-halo zone, measured in the *complementary* observable, which makes it the sharpest available external constraint on whether VIRUS fiber stacks at 100–350 kpc are dominated by a real CGM signal or by the background treatment. It is also the closest thing in print to a validation of the 80–90″ annulus question, from a team that had to solve the same problem with the same fibers.

2. **Laishram 2026** — Discovery of a z ≃ 4.9 Lyman-α Emitter Protocluster: Wavelength-Dependent Environmental Effects on Galaxy Structure (arXiv:2603.03570, March 2026; ApJL). Finds an LAE protocluster at z = 4.90 **in the COSMOS field** with four overdensity peaks over ~65 × 36 cMpc² and a 4× surface-density enhancement within 1.5 pMpc, characterizing environment with **two independent estimators — fifth-nearest-neighbour distance and Voronoi tessellation** — and reports that protocluster LAEs differ in extended stellar structure but *not* in rest-UV size. Two contacts with Paper II's plan to build its own multi-scale density field rather than only importing Chartab 2020: it is a worked example of estimator choice being a stated methodological decision rather than a default, and the wavelength-dependence result is a caution that "environment affects the extended component but not the compact one" is a pattern that shows up at other redshifts — which is the VDFI thesis (one-halo tracks the galaxy, two-halo tracks the environment) appearing in a structural rather than emission measurement.

**Older, likely not yet reviewed:**

3. **Infante-Sainz 2020** — The Sloan Digital Sky Survey extended point spread functions (arXiv:1911.01430, November 2019; MNRAS 491, 5317). Constructs extended PSFs for all five SDSS bands out to ~8 arcmin by **combining stars in separate magnitude ranges** — bright saturated stars for the outer wings, progressively fainter unsaturated stars for the inner region — and splicing them into one composite curve, with explicit treatment of saturation, ghosts and the sky pedestal. This is the same construction `build_piecewise_psf` implements independently, and it is worth reading for two specific things the docstring leaves open: how they set the splice radii (VDFI currently reads them off the plot by hand into `valid_radius`) and how they handled the sky term, which is exactly the additive error the VDFI function warns cannot be divided out by the Gaia normalization. If the piecewise PSF is going in Methods, this is the natural citation for the technique and the natural check that the protocol is standard rather than bespoke.

4. **Dong 2024** — The Effect of AGN Feedback on the Lyman-α Forest Signature of Galaxy Protoclusters at z ~ 2.3 (arXiv:2402.13568, February 2024; MNRAS 532, 4876). Uses SIMBA and The Three Hundred to predict large-scale Lyα *transmission* around protoclusters at 2 < z < 2.5, then compares against **seven protoclusters in the COSMOS field** — finding only two show the expected excess absorption, four are anomalously transparent in a way matching AGN X-ray thermal feedback, and one appears to reflect jet feedback. Directly relevant to the standing position that COSMOS ≠ EGS is physical rather than a systematic, and to the layout's §5 "Outside" discriminator: it says the IGM state inside COSMOS's known overdensities is *not* a simple function of density, which is a complication for reading an outer Lyα amplitude straight off a density field, and a reason to prefer the within-COSMOS split over a cross-field comparison. Also a useful counterweight to importing COSTCO as a clean environmental label.
