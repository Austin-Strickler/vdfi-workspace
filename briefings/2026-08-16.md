## Literature scan

Checked against all 66 filenames in `docs/literature/` and against the standing suggestions carried in the 07-10 through 08-10 briefings (Arrigoni Battaia 2016, Bennett 2026, Carr 2025, Cerdosino 2025, Claeyssens 2022, Davis 2023, Feldmeier 2013, Gagnon-Hartman 2026, Gallego 2021, Hall 2026, Li 2025, Matsuda 2012, Roy 2026, Runnholm 2023, Saldana-Lopez 2026, Sebastian 2026, Smirnov 2026, Verhamme 2018, Weiss 2024, Wu 2020, Xue 2017, Yu 2025, Zhang 2026 (LAMOST), Zhang & Zaritsky 2024 — none re-listed). Weighted toward the question the `f_esc` > 1 falsification opened: **if the two-halo term isn't in-situ star formation, what is it, and how does anyone else measure it?**

**Recent:**

1. **Lujan Niemeyer 2026** — Lyα Intensity Mapping in HETDEX: Galaxy–Lyα Intensity Cross-power Spectrum (arXiv:2510.11427, ApJ, 2026). Measures the cross-power spectrum between HETDEX galaxy positions and the diffuse Lyα intensity field, i.e. the same large-scale Lyα-around-galaxies signal VDFI measures in configuration space, on the same instrument, in Fourier space. Byrohl is a co-author, which makes the simulation-side interpretation explicit rather than inferred. This is the most directly comparable published measurement of the term the two-halo budget work just falsified an origin for, and it is the same first author as the Tier 1 external-replication check — the natural place to state what the two measurements do and don't share.

2. **Li & Zheng 2025** — Emulating extended Lyman-alpha haloes around star-forming galaxies (arXiv:2508.10095, MNRAS, August 2025). Builds Gaussian-process emulators over Lyα radiative-transfer simulations of a parametrized CGM (density, velocity, temperature, with self-shielding against the UV background), predicting surface-brightness profiles separately for **three** source terms: central star formation, **satellite galaxies**, and **in-CGM recombination**. That decomposition is exactly the alternative set left standing after the photon budget killed in-situ case-B star formation, and the emulator makes it fittable rather than argued. *One flag: an earlier briefing carried a standing "Li 2025" suggestion — check whether it is this paper before adding it.*

**Older, likely not yet reviewed:**

3. **Gronke 2015** — A systematic study of Lyα transfer through outflowing shells: model parameter estimation (arXiv:1506.03836, Gronke, Bull & Dijkstra, ApJ 812, 123, 2015). Fits shell-model RT to large grids of synthetic spectra and characterizes when the model parameters are and aren't recoverable. Worth filing because `GOALS.md` §8 item 2 already leans on this paper by name for the project's position that the shell-model parameter space is unconstrainable from a stacked, medium-resolution, single-line measurement — a load-bearing citation that isn't in `docs/literature/`, which is a gap to close before it appears in a Methods section.

4. **Kikuchihara 2022** — Lyα intensity mapping of the LAE–Lyα cross-correlation out to ~1 cMpc (*arXiv ID not confirmed by this scan; search by author and title*). Detects diffuse Lyα around LAEs on scales well beyond the halo by cross-correlating narrowband intensity with LAE positions. Relevant for the same reason as item 1 and by an independent technique: it is a positive detection of large-scale Lyα that carries no assumption about in-situ star formation, so it constrains the two-halo amplitude from a direction the photon budget can't reach. Pair it with Kakuma's earlier version of the same measurement if the pair is easy to pull together.
